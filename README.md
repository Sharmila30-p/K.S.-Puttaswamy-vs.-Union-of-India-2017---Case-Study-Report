# K.S.Puttaswamy vs.Union of India (2017) - Case Study Report

This project presents a case study of the landmark K.S. Puttaswamy vs. Union of India (2017) case, which recognised the right to privacy as a fundamental right under the Indian Constitution. The report covers the facts, legal issues, Supreme Court judgement, and key legal principles related to privacy, personal liberty, dignity, personal choices, and protection of personal information. It also explains the importance of the case for businesses, especially banks, technology companies, and digital platforms that collect and manage customer data.
